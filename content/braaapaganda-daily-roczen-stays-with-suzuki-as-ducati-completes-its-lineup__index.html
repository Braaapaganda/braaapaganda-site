Braaapaganda Daily: Roczen Stays With Suzuki as Ducati Completes Its Lineup

October 30, 2026

Two pieces of the 2027 450 picture are now clearer: Ken Roczen is staying with PMG Suzuki, while Joey Savatgy joins RJ Hampshire at Troy Lee Designs Red Bull Ducati Factory Racing. The distinction matters. Suzuki is keeping a title-winning partnership together; Ducati is filling out the rider lineup for its developing program.

Roczen chooses continuity

Suzuki and PMG's announcement, circulated Thursday, confirms Roczen will return on the RM-Z450 for the 2027 AMA Supercross season. Suzuki's own Friday announcement puts the number-one plate at the center of the story. This keeps the defending champion with a familiar team rather than turning his next season into a machinery switch. Sources: Suzuki and PMG announcement via Vital MX and Suzuki's announcement.

There is an important boundary to the announcement: a confirmed Supercross title defense does not automatically establish a full outdoor schedule. MotoOnline's October 30 report says the contract's exact duration and Roczen's Pro Motocross participation have not been detailed. Those remain open questions, not blanks to fill with assumptions. Read MotoOnline's report.

Our take: the immediate value is continuity. Familiar equipment and personnel remove some adaptation work from a championship defense. That is an advantage in preparation, not a prediction that the next title is already decided.

Savatgy gives Ducati a complete pairing

Thursday's Ducati announcement confirms a multi-year deal for Savatgy alongside Hampshire. The official series release describes a full Supercross, Pro Motocross and SMX program. This is a broader stated racing commitment than the Supercross-only scope currently confirmed for Roczen. Sources: Pro Motocross announcement and the original team announcement.

For Ducati, having both seats identified gives its project a clearer shape. The next useful evidence will be how the pairing translates into testing, race readiness and results. A completed lineup is a concrete step; it is not yet proof of competitive parity with established programs.

The media conversation

Racer X and Vital MX carried the Roczen announcement, while MotoOnline added the useful distinction between the confirmed title defense and unresolved outdoor plans. Their reproductions of team announcements are not separate confirmations of facts originating in the same release. MotoOnline also published an October 30 Roczen interview page from Auckland. We reviewed its written introduction, not the video, and are not attributing unseen comments to him. Read the interview introduction.

What to watch next

Look for an explicit Roczen outdoor plan, additional detail on Ducati's preparation, and official changes to the 2027 competition framework.
