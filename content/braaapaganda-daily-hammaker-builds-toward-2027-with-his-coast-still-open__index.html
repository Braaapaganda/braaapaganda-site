Braaapaganda Daily: Hammaker Builds Toward 2027 With His Coast Still Open

A first-person recovery update, preparations for the next SMX broadcast season, and a clear line between Sexton predictions and a signed deal.

November 14, 2026

Seth Hammaker has offered a concrete update on his 2027 preparations. Elsewhere, the SMX broadcast team is working on next season’s presentation, while Chase Sexton’s destination remains a prediction rather than a confirmed signing in the reporting reviewed for this edition.

Hammaker: back on Supercross tracks, coast undecided

In a November 13 written interview with Racer X, Hammaker says he is riding Supercross in Florida and building his workload with Michael Byrne after the shoulder injury he suffered at High Point in June. He puts his shoulder’s strength and mobility at roughly 80 percent and says it feels stable.

That is the rider’s own assessment, not independent medical clearance. His East or West assignment is still undecided. He is open to either region, with readiness helping guide the team’s decision, and his stated target remains a championship.

Why it matters: This is a preparation update, not an entry-list announcement. The next useful milestone is a confirmed regional assignment and further evidence of sustained training. For now, a percentage quoted in an interview should not become a precise forecast of race fitness.

Inside the broadcast planning

In November 13’s Racerhead, Jason Weigandt reports attending a two-day planning summit involving the SMX broadcast operation, NBC, Feld and MX Sports. He says the group studied Olympic coverage and recorded material for a season-preview program expected just after Christmas.

It is an on-the-record account from a participant, not a published schedule or a guarantee of particular production changes. It gives viewers something specific to watch for: what the preview reveals about how next season will be presented.

Sexton: commentary is still not a contract

In that same Racerhead column, Davey Coombs argues that Quad Lock Honda looks like a fit for Sexton. That is his assessment. It does not announce an agreement, and we are not converting it into one.

What to watch next

The strongest offseason updates change something verifiable: a rider confirms his program, a team identifies its lineup, or an organizer publishes a schedule. Until then, we will keep preparation, predictions and confirmed commitments in separate lanes.
