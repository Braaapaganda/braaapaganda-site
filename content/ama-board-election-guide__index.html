Before you plan a run

Candidate requirements stated by AMA staff

Staff described both parts below. You need the membership history AND one of the education or experience qualifications.

  • Part 1 / Membership

    At least five consecutive years

    At least five consecutive years of AMA membership.

  • AND part 2 / Education or experience

    Bachelor’s degree OR managerial experience

    Either a bachelor’s degree OR several years of managerial experience in a professional capacity.

Source: AMA correspondence, October 22, 2026. Governing bylaws/election policies have not yet been obtained; this is not a complete independently verified eligibility checklist. These are candidate requirements, not established voting requirements.

Confirm your regional eligibility and the full, current candidate requirements directly with the AMA before applying.

The part that trips people up

Apply first. Voting comes later.

The year on the cards means the application cycle. A contested vote can continue into January of the following year.

  1. 01

    Candidate applications

    Watch the official election page and summer issues of American Motorcyclist. Use the deadline in that year’s notice.

  2. 02

    Member voting, if contested

    Recent ballots have run from December into January. Dates vary by cycle. An uncontested seat can be retained without a competitive ballot.

  3. 03

    Outcome announced

    A January result may belong to the previous year’s application cycle. Don’t wait for a voting notice if you want to run.

A real example: North Central voting opened December 16, 2024 and closed January 15, 2025. That was the 2024 cycle, not a new 2025 application opportunity.

Common questions

Is this the complete eligibility checklist?

No. The staff-reported candidate requirements above are guidance, not a complete independently verified bylaw or election-policy checklist. Confirm the full requirements and your regional eligibility with the AMA.

Which region am I in?

Use the source-qualified state lists beside the projected application cycles above. These are AMA board-election regions, not racing qualifier regions. California and Illinois are split between regions; exact county cutlines have not been verified. Published AMA notices also differ on Alaska and Nevada. Confirm the region assigned to your membership address directly with AMA, especially in split states, Alaska and Nevada.

Why are the future years marked “projected”?

The three-year pattern is supported by recent election results and the current directory. The next application notices and exact dates have not been located. Southeast’s 2029 year also has AMA staff support, but that is not a published application notice.

Does “no current elections” mean nobody could run?

No. The 2026 candidate period closed with no challengers, and the incumbents retained their seats. The application deadline matters even when there is no competitive vote afterward.
