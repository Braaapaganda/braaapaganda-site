Our platform / Proposed rider care

No Rider
Left Behind.

The sport’s responsibility should not end when an injured rider leaves the track.

This is a Braaapaganda proposal, not current AMA policy or an operating assistance program. It requires formal adoption, protected funding and an agreed delivery plan. No existing partnerships, insurance coverage or compensation are promised.

The care proposal ↓Work with existing organizations ↓Reach out or tell your story ↓

Care that continues after race day.

Four commitments for a funded rider-support program, with clear eligibility and meaningful follow-through.

  • A guide through the aftermath

    One injury navigator to help a rider understand insurance processes, organize claims and find appropriate support, with agreed next steps and follow-up.

  • Funded recovery & family care

    A dedicated, transparently funded program for rehabilitation and practical family support, with published eligibility and clear limits.

  • Learn from serious incidents

    Independent serious-incident review focused on what happened and how to improve safety, kept separate from assistance decisions and general medical files.

  • Stay present, with consent

    Ongoing contact on the rider’s terms. Any public update or visibility requires the rider’s informed, continuing consent. No forced publicity.

Build on the help already out there

Work with established organizations.

The AMA should actively seek out and work with established rider-support organizations, injury charities and recovery services. Strengthen their work, coordinate referrals and fund unmet needs rather than duplicate effective programs.

  1. Map the support

    Identify services, eligibility, capacity and a reliable contact at each willing organization.

  2. Make the connection

    With the rider’s permission, the navigator makes a direct referral and shares only agreed information.

  3. Close the gaps

    Check whether help was available. Identify unmet needs and coordinate funded support without making the rider start over.

  4. Stay accountable

    Agree responsibilities, protect independence and report privacy-protected service gaps and spending.

Partners keep their independence. Each charity or provider retains its own eligibility rules, clinical decisions, capacity and funding limits. A referral is not guaranteed acceptance or funding. This proposal does not claim any partnership already exists.

Rider first. Always.

Your care.
Your choice.
Your privacy.

  • Help must not depend on a story or a complaint. Under this proposal, support would never require an interview, public praise, a forum post or an integrity complaint.
  • Permission is specific, not a blank check. Agree what may be shared, with whom and why. Riders can decline referrals or future publicity without losing access to other eligible support.
  • Separate care from investigations. Assistance needs its own staff, funding, eligibility and consent. General medical files must not be combined with integrity case files.
  • Fund the plan before making the promise. Publish scope, eligibility, service standards, privacy protections and independent oversight before launch. Navigation cannot guarantee insurance payments or compensation.
