Braaapaganda Daily: Swoll Lands a Multi-Year Phoenix Honda Deal

Phoenix’s November 12 announcement gives Jalek Swoll a 2027 destination and makes patience part of the plan.

November 13, 2026

Jalek Swoll has his next ride. Phoenix Racing Honda announced a multi-year agreement on November 12, beginning in 2027. The plan puts him on a CRF250R for 250SX East, Pro Motocross and SMX. That is the meaningful change since yesterday’s edition: a confirmed domestic team move, rather than another round of offseason speculation.

What Phoenix and Swoll are saying

Phoenix’s announcement, reproduced by Racer X, emphasizes time and support. Swoll says he values the team’s willingness not to rush his progress after injury interruptions. Team manager Gary Schlentz describes investment in staff, processes and equipment, alongside a patient approach to Swoll’s return to his best.

Racer X also reports that the move reunites Swoll with technical director Steve Westfall, who previously worked with him at Husqvarna and Triumph. The agreement establishes a destination and a racing program. It does not give us a current medical assessment or a guaranteed level of performance.

Why the length of the deal matters

Our read: A multi-year commitment gives both sides room to judge the partnership across more than an opening race. For a rider changing bikes, that matters: learning the equipment and building race consistency are separate jobs, and neither is settled by an announcement.

The useful question now is whether Phoenix can turn its stated patience into a stable preparation period. Results will be the eventual measure, but the first things to watch are straightforward: sustained riding, clear updates from Swoll and the team, and how the Honda package develops before the East campaign. A longer deal creates an opportunity; it is not evidence that those steps are already complete.

The media conversation

MotoOnline’s November 12 report also centers the Honda switch and East-region plan. Much of the coverage draws on the same team announcement. Multiple outlets carrying those statements should not be counted as separate evidence for every claim inside them.

For now, the strongest story is the confirmed move and the approach both sides say they want to take. We are leaving unreviewed social-video remarks out of the account and will judge the racing when there is racing to judge.
