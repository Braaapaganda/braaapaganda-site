Braaapaganda Daily: Mosiman’s Honda move sharpens the 2027 picture

Michael Mosiman has a confirmed home for 2027. His move to Quad Lock Honda is today's significant addition to a reshaped 250 field, while the wider American MX/SX/SMX conversation remains focused on offseason preparation and the next season's lineups.

October 22, 2026

October 22, 2026 · Research cutoff: 3:36 p.m. Eastern / 19:36 UTC

Michael Mosiman has a confirmed home for 2027. His move to Quad Lock Honda is today’s significant addition to a reshaped 250 field, while the wider American MX/SX/SMX conversation remains focused on offseason preparation and the next season’s lineups.

Today’s development: Mosiman joins Quad Lock Honda

Quad Lock Honda has announced Mosiman for its 250 program across AMA Supercross, Pro Motocross and the SMX postseason. Racer X published the team’s release on October 22. Mosiman brings an AMA 250SX main-event win and a third-place finish in the 2022 West championship to the program.

The release also describes increased American Honda support and a role for Kevin Windham spanning rider development, testing and performance. Mosiman identifies Windham’s involvement as an important reason for choosing the team. Those are meaningful details of the announced program; the team’s championship ambitions still have to be tested on track. Quad Lock Honda release via Racer X, October 22

Our read: the interesting question now is how Mosiman and the new support structure turn preparation into consistent race results. Today’s announcement gives that story a concrete starting point.

The 250 field around him

Pro Circuit Kawasaki’s plans were confirmed on October 19. Nate Thrasher joins Levi Kitchen, Seth Hammaker, Drew Adams and Nick Romano in a five-rider roster. Kitchen returns as the reigning 250SMX champion, while Romano moves into a full-time role after filling in during 2026. That puts an established championship winner, another incoming race winner and developing riders under the same roof. How that depth translates across the season will be worth following. Pro Motocross, October 19

KTM’s October 14 announcement confirms a four-rider 250 program: Daxton Bennick, Ryder DiFrancesco, Julien Beaumer and Sacha Coenen, managed by Nathan Ramsey. Its factory 450 pairing is Jorge Prado and Lucas Coenen. These are earlier announcements that help explain today’s landscape, rather than additional signings that happened this afternoon. Pro Motocross, October 14

The offseason conversation

The official Supercross site posted an AC & JB episode on October 21 about team changes and expectations for the offseason. Vital MX’s current silly-season show likewise centers on transfers, including RJ Hampshire, Justin Barcia and Thrasher. These are places to follow the discussion around the changing grid. The episode descriptions do not establish additional signings beyond the announcements themselves. AC & JB · Vital MX

Where the next evidence comes from

Justin Barcia is confirmed for the November 7 AUSX Open at GMHBA Stadium in Geelong on his new Twisted Tea Suzuki presented by Progressive Insurance. The organizer’s release, published by Racer X on October 20, also names Ken Roczen in the international SX1 field. It gives fans an upcoming race appearance to watch as Barcia gets established on the Suzuki. Racer X / AUSX release

For the U.S. championship, Anaheim 1 opens AMA Supercross at Angel Stadium on January 9, 2027. Pro Motocross begins at Pala Raceway Presented by Fox Racing on May 29. Until then, the useful markers are official roster confirmations, documented rule or calendar changes, and actual racing that shows how the new combinations work. Anaheim event · Pala update
