Braaapaganda Daily: Quad Lock Confirms Sexton Talks

Konsky discusses Honda negotiations, AUSX changes its race formats, and WMX sets its 2027 calendar.

November 6, 2026

Chase Sexton’s 2027 search has a firmer development: Quad Lock Honda owner Yarrive Konsky says talks are taking place. That leads today’s Daily, alongside a format change in Australian Supercross and a confirmed women’s motocross schedule.

Sexton and Quad Lock: talks confirmed, decision still open

In Alex Gobert’s November 5 report for MotoOnline, Konsky acknowledges discussions about signing Sexton. The team owner says other teams are also in the picture and Sexton has yet to announce a decision.

A named team owner has acknowledged negotiations. The reporting reviewed for this edition does not establish a completed signing. A return to Honda remains a possibility; the next meaningful step would be a confirmed roster announcement.

AUSX adds a third final at Perth and Newcastle

Kane Taylor’s November 6 MotoOnline report says the rest of the Australian Supercross season will use Triple Crown formats. AUSX CEO Adam Bailey says the decision combines the entertainment value of three finals with giving full-series riders more chances to recover points from visiting stars.

Perth on November 14 and Newcastle on November 28 had been planned as Double Headers. Geelong already uses the Triple Crown format. This is an Australian series change, not a new AMA Supercross format announcement.

WMX’s six-round 2027 schedule is official

In its November 4 announcement, the SMX League set six WMX weekends alongside Pro Motocross: Pala May 28–29, Hangtown June 4–5, Thunder Valley June 11–12, Unadilla August 13–14, Budds Creek August 20–21, and Ironman August 27–28.

The WMX Invitational returns at the SMX Final on September 25. Its venue had not been announced in the release. These are event weekends, not separate confirmation of each WMX session’s day or time.

The AMA rulebook gets a follow-up

The published description of Jason Weigandt’s November 5 Weege Show for Racer X introduces a discussion with the AMA’s Jeremy Albrecht about the 2027 rules, including sound limits, 20-rider main events and lapped traffic. This is follow-up coverage of the already-released rules.

Section 2.3.4 of the AMA’s 2027 Supercross rulebook specifies a 109 dB/A pre-race sound limit and a 110 dB/A post-race limit using the two-meter-max method. Riders and teams should consult the complete section for testing requirements.

Worth watching

The official description of the November 5 SMX Insider episode lists offseason team changes and Haiden Deegan’s place in the 450 conversation. It is a viewing pointer for analysis, not evidence of an additional signing.

Coverage note: The MotoOnline items are attributed reporting from named participants. Video segments are identified from their published descriptions; their audio and video were not reviewed for this edition. No Sexton destination is presented as settled.
