Braaapaganda Daily: Russell Wins Ironman; Sexton Watch Continues

Russell wins Ironman, three GNCC contenders sit within 11 points, and the Sexton discussion continues without a confirmed next-team announcement.

October 26, 2026

Monday, October 26, 2026 • Research cutoff: 9:12 a.m. Eastern (13:12 UTC).

The Supercross and Pro Motocross news cycle is quiet this morning. The fresh result worth your attention comes from Ironman GNCC, where Kailub Russell won and the overall championship remains tightly packed. Meanwhile, the Chase Sexton conversation is still running ahead of a confirmed next-team announcement.

This is a shorter Daily: new results first, older roster context clearly labeled, and no recycled rumor presented as a signing.

Ironman: Russell wins, three riders within 11 points

Russell took the overall victory ahead of Liam Draper and Michael Witkowski. Championship leader Jordan Ashburn finished sixth, one place ahead of Steward Baylor.

The organizer-linked GNCC results and standings now put Ashburn on 202 points, Baylor on 193 and Draper on 191. Those figures agree with Racer X’s Monday Wake-Up Call, published this morning.

Why it matters: Ashburn still has the lead, but Draper’s runner-up finish keeps the top three within 11 points heading toward the final round. That is a real championship development, even on a weekend without a national motocross or Supercross main event.

Racer X also reports an XC2 victory for Jhak Walker ahead of Jason Tino and Toby Cleveland, with Walker and Angus Riordan tied on 251 points. In WXC, its results list Brandy Richards ahead of Danielle McDonald and Korie Steede. Those class results are attributed to Racer X; the independent check for this edition covered the overall result and overall championship table.

Sexton: the departure is confirmed; the destination is not

Older context, October 23–24: Kawasaki and Sexton’s immediate split was already covered in our previous editions. MotoOnline’s report remains the useful starting point for what was actually announced.

The sources checked for this edition did not establish a new team signing. That distinction matters when evaluating the current wave of Suzuki and Yamaha discussion: a reported possibility, an analyst’s preferred fit and a completed deal are different things.

The media conversation: MotoOnline’s “What’s Next for Chase Sexton?” podcast description frames the question around his next move. Johnny Hopper’s October 24 video description likewise discusses possible options and explicitly identifies some material as rumor and opinion. Chris Cooksey’s October 25 upload continued the discussion, but the material reviewed supplied no team announcement. We reviewed accessible descriptions and posts, not full transcripts of those videos.

AM Open: keep the replay separate from the results sheet

Yesterday’s Daily covered the revised Sunday schedule and viewing links for the AM Open in Arizona. The event’s linked results service was not readable during today’s check, so this edition is not naming class winners from an unverified list or inferring them from a broadcast title.

That is a verification gap, not a claim that results do not exist. InsiderMX’s AM Open hub lists Sunday racing and championship-podium coverage for anyone catching up.

What to watch next

  • A direct Sexton announcement: a rider or team statement identifying the next program would materially change the story.
  • Readable AM Open final results: those are needed before a reliable class-by-class wrap-up.
  • The actual 2027 Supercross rules documents: the AMA rules page displays a CB2027.01.SX rule-update heading, but the underlying document and a release date were not available through that page during this check. No specific rule change is asserted here.

Source check: Racer X, Vital MX, MotoOnline US, Swapmoto Live, PulpMX, Cooksey and Hopper’s verified channels; Feld, Supercross, SMX, AMA, Pro Motocross and MX Sports news/bulletin pages. No newly dated October 25–26 official SX/MX/SMX news release was found in the listings reviewed. Earlier announcements and commentary above are dated accordingly.
