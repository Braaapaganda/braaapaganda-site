Australia Is Paying Supercross Riders to Make Content. Why Isn't America?

November 18, 2026 · Public episode

Rob looks at AUSX’s rider-content rewards and privateer support, and argues that American Supercross should learn from Australia’s approach.

The episode covers AUSX’s announced Content Rewards Program, offering up to A$10,000 per approved rider, alongside its dedicated Privateer Championship, lower entry fees, reduced-cost freight and discounted accommodation. The content reward is an earning opportunity, not a guaranteed payment.
