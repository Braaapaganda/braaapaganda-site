Braaapaganda Daily: Mini O’s Switches to Motocross

The Gatorback outdoor races begin, the 250 Pro Sport Supercross result needs a final check, and Road 2 Recovery’s auction opens.

November 25, 2026

The THOR Mini O’s moves from Supercross to outdoor motocross today at Gatorback Cycle Park. There’s still a loose end from the SX mains: later coverage lists a different 250 Pro Sport podium than the first race report. Yesterday’s roundup called a winner from that initial account. That was too definitive without the final class sheet.

What changed in the Supercross reporting

Vurbmoto’s November 24 report and MX1Onboard’s November 25 report disagree on who won 250 Pro Sport. Show Radical’s rider-by-rider recap agrees with the later report on second place, but that doesn’t establish the whole podium. We couldn’t independently verify the final class sheet at cutoff. The November 24 edition should not be read as the final result.

The other class to track is 250 A. MX1Onboard reports Landon Gibson, Kane Bollasina and Grant McDonald in that order; that’s an attributed result, not a confirmed list of SMX qualifiers. The SMX League’s November 3 announcement says the top three in 250 A at Mini O’s can earn places in the following year’s SMX Next field, but it doesn’t specify the discipline at Gatorback. The promoter’s published race orders list 250 A in Supercross and not in motocross. The league still needs to confirm which riders are in the 2027 field. A place in that field is not a direct ticket to the postseason.

Where to follow the outdoor races

FloRacing’s event schedule had practice at 7 a.m. Eastern today, with racing to follow. Outdoor races run through Saturday. FloRacing’s event hub has live and replay listings, and its timing guide points to Prospect for class results. A Supercross class win doesn’t settle the week’s Olympiad: the promoter combines the SX main and motocross overall in classes that run both.

Prime Video separately lists a 1 p.m. Eastern stream for both Wednesday and Thursday, each marked free to watch. Those are viewing windows, not race start times. The listings don’t say which classes will be shown, and playback was not tested.

Also today: Road 2 Recovery’s auction

Road 2 Recovery lists its holiday auction from November 25 through December 5. Its release published by Racer X puts the opening at 2 p.m. Eastern today. The MX Locker catalog includes signed gear and Supercross experiences. The nonprofit says proceeds go to injured athletes’ funds.

In the media

In its November 24 episode notes, PulpMX lists Jeremy Albrecht on JGR and the AMA, Chris Blose on life after racing, and Seth Rarick on Top Gun MX. Those are the show’s listed topics, not new rulings or announcements.

Research cutoff: November 25, 2026, 8:33 a.m. EST (13:33 UTC).
