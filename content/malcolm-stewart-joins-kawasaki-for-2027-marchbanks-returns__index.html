Malcolm Stewart Joins Kawasaki for 2027; Marchbanks Returns

Malcolm Stewart joins Monster Energy Kawasaki for 2027 alongside returning Garrett Marchbanks, giving the team a confirmed two-rider SMX roster.

October 28, 2026

Malcolm Stewart is officially joining Monster Energy Kawasaki for 2027. Kawasaki confirmed the signing Wednesday, October 28, with Garrett Marchbanks returning after a contract extension. The two-rider SMX roster will race the new KX450F-SR. Read Kawasaki’s announcement via Supercross Live.

The news arrives five days after Kawasaki and Chase Sexton mutually ended their partnership, effective October 23. It settles Kawasaki’s lineup, but not Sexton’s next move: Racer X’s reporting this morning still identifies his destination as unknown.

Stewart gets a new home

The move brings Stewart back to a brand he rode as an amateur, according to Kawasaki’s announcement.

“I’m very excited to be joining Monster Energy Kawasaki for 2027,” Stewart said in the team release.

There is a race-winning benchmark behind the signing. Stewart earned his first 450SX victory at Tampa on February 8, 2025, charging forward from a mid-pack start in his 109th premier-class Supercross appearance. Cooper Webb finished second and Jason Anderson third. The official race recap captures the performance that put Stewart on the 450SX winners list.

Marchbanks stays to build on 2026

Marchbanks returns as the 2026 450SX Rookie of the Year. Kawasaki’s announcement credits him with fourth-overall finishes at Thunder Valley and Unadilla, plus sixth overall in the SMX World Championship standings. His extension keeps a developing rider in the program rather than starting over with two newcomers.

Our read: the lineup is settled; the results are not

Stewart brings evidence that he can win a premier-class Supercross main event. Marchbanks brings recent experience within Kawasaki’s operation. That makes this an interesting pairing, but neither credential tells us how quickly they will find a competitive setup together.

The useful questions now move to preparation: how Stewart adapts, whether Marchbanks can make his best finishes more regular, and how both respond to the new motorcycle. Those are things to judge through testing reports and racing, not the confidence of a signing announcement.

The 2027 Supercross opener is scheduled for January 9 at Angel Stadium in Anaheim. Kawasaki has named its riders. The next job is turning that lineup into results.

Source note: Kawasaki’s release, carried by Supercross Live and Racer X, is one originating announcement. Racer X’s additional reporting is attributed separately. Sources published before 12:15 p.m. EDT / 16:15 UTC on October 28, 2026.
