Braaapaganda Daily: Prueitt Wins Mini O’s 250 Pro Sport Supercross

Tristan Prueitt tops Monday’s 250 Pro Sport main, AEO sets its 250 West lineup, and Pro Motocross confirms when full ticket options open.

November 24, 2026

Tristan Prueitt won Monday’s 250 Pro Sport Supercross main at the THOR Mini O’s, ahead of Kane Bollasina and Landon Gibson, according to Vurbmoto, which runs the event’s Prospect timing and results service. Keep two results separate at Gatorback: 250 Pro Sport counts toward the AMA’s Supercross licensing pathway, but 250 A is the class the SMX League named for its 2027 development program.

One race, three riders at different stages

Prueitt’s win comes three months after MX Sports reported his AMA Amateur Rider of the Year award at Loretta Lynn’s. Gibson took the Horizon Award at that same event and signed with Triumph in October. Bollasina was fifth in the September SMX Next playoff standings. Monday put the Loretta’s amateur award winner ahead of two riders who already had SMX Next playoff experience. That’s a useful offseason result, not a forecast for Anaheim.

Vurbmoto also reported Chase Andersen’s 250 B Limited win, describing a late lead change after Ryder Ellis caught lapped traffic. Its earlier Duff–Bollasina clip was from the opening 250 Pro Sport moto, not the main. Don’t read the opening moto as the final order.

Why 250 A is a separate result to watch

The AMA’s 2026 Road to Supercross bulletin awards two points for a top-ten overall in either 250 A or 250 Pro Sport on the Mini O’s Supercross track. That’s credit toward a license, not a license by itself. The SMX League’s November 3 announcement separately names a top-three 250 A route at Mini O’s for next season’s SMX Next field. The release doesn’t specify which discipline at Gatorback supplies that classification. The 250 Pro Sport finish alone does not settle who earned those spots.

AEO puts all three riders in the 250 West

AEO’s team site and a November 23 Husqvarna release published by Racer X put Carson Mumford, Benjamin Garib and Leo Tucker on the factory-supported program for 250SX West and outdoor 250s. The Husqvarna switch was announced in October. The detail to file now is that all three are slated to be on the same coast, starting January 9 at Anaheim 1. Mumford was seventh in the 2026 outdoor 250 standings. Garib brings international racing experience, and Tucker told the team that making his first Supercross night show in Seattle was a step, not where he wants to stop. The next benchmark is whether he can turn those night-show appearances into main-event starts.

What to watch next

Unlimited Sports MX’s schedule puts motocross practice after Tuesday’s remaining SX mains and the first outdoor races on Wednesday. Keep the SX results, outdoor results and combined Olympiad awards separate. FloRacing has the broadcast schedule; its timing guide points to Prospect for the class sheets. Prime Video lists a free Tuesday stream from 8 a.m. Eastern. That’s a separate viewing window, not the start of racing; its class-by-class coverage was not verified.

For next summer, Pro Motocross announced Monday that full ticket options for all 11 Nationals open to the public Friday, November 27. Some tickets were already on sale; Friday’s change is the full selection, including general admission, VIP and camping options.

Research cutoff: November 24, 2026, 8:29 a.m. EST (13:29 UTC). The 250 Pro Sport order is attributed to Vurbmoto; the full class sheet was not independently available at cutoff.
