Braaapaganda Daily: AM Open Finals Sunday, With a Revised Start Time

AM Open Sunday practice now starts at 9 a.m. Arizona time, with finals afterward. Find the revised race order, free coverage and Saturday replays.

October 25, 2026

October 25, 2026 | Research cutoff: 11:03 a.m. Eastern (15:03 UTC)

The AM Open of Supercross moves into its Sunday main-event program at Arizona Cycle Park, and the useful update is the start time. The organizer’s Sunday schedule, marked updated October 24, now calls for three-lap practice at 9 a.m. Arizona time, noon Eastern (16:00 UTC). Main events follow practice. Do not plan around the older 8 a.m. listing, and do not mistake the practice start for a fixed first-main start. Official Sunday schedule

What to watch for Sunday

The published order has 27 motos, beginning with 250 Pro Sport. Open Pro Sport is Moto 16, following a scheduled 20-minute track-preparation break. Both Pro Sport races are listed at ten laps; 250 B, Open B and College run seven, while the remaining classes run five. Each moto gets one sight lap. Some gates combine classes, but those classes retain separate results. Check the organizer’s updates before heading to staging or tuning in. Moto order and race lengths

Saturday is available to catch up

InsiderMX’s updated AM Open hub now links Sunday racing and championship-podium coverage alongside Saturday’s full racing and podium replays. Its class finder also lists 27 qualifying-race selections, making it easier to find a particular gate without searching through the full broadcast. Coverage is free. Sunday coverage and Saturday archive

Saturday’s official results remain unverified at cutoff. The event-linked results page was unavailable during our earlier check. The replay listings above are viewing options, not a race recap; this edition does not name qualifying winners.

A quiet pro-news morning
