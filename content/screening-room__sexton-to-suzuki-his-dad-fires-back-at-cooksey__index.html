Sexton to Suzuki? His Dad Fires Back at Cooksey

October 26, 2026

Watch the video ↗

Chase Sexton’s rumored Suzuki move has turned into a public clash between Chris Cooksey and Sexton’s dad. We break down Cooksey’s report, the Instagram response, and the bigger question: does Chase need a different bike—or a different environment?

I explain why the proposed Suzuki setup could make sense, why I’d like to see what a structured Star Yamaha program could do for Chase, and how his ACL recovery complicates the Anaheim 1 outlook.

Plus: our proposal for transparent, independent reviews of racing-integrity concerns, triggered by 100 unique AMA members raising the same incident within 14 days. This is a proposed reform, not an existing AMA rule. Championship-manipulation allegations discussed in this episode remain unproven.

The Sexton–Suzuki deal and alleged conditions attached to other team offers are unconfirmed reporting, not an official signing announcement.
