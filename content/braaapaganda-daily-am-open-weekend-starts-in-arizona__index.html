Braaapaganda Daily: AM Open weekend starts in Arizona

October 23, 2026

October 23, 2026 · Research cutoff: 8:35 a.m. Eastern / 12:35 UTC

The most useful item on Friday’s calendar is in Buckeye, Arizona: the Amateur Open of Supercross starts its October 23–25 weekend. For the national pro scene, this is a quieter morning. We have not verified another major U.S. MX/SX/SMX roster, rule or race-result development in the accessible sources checked since yesterday’s edition, so today’s rundown is shorter rather than padded with recycled news.

Racing to follow this weekend

The AM Open is at Arizona Cycle Park. This is a happening-today reminder, not a newly announced event: the organizer’s weekend guide was published October 13.

The current schedule lists:

  • Friday, October 23: organized practice, 9 a.m.–2 p.m. Arizona time.
  • Saturday, October 24: practice and qualifying program begins at 8 a.m.
  • Sunday, October 25: A and B main-event program begins at 8 a.m.

Arizona is on Mountain Standard Time here, three hours behind Eastern. That makes Friday’s practice start noon Eastern and the weekend program starts 11 a.m. Eastern. Times and class orders can change; use the official schedule and Friday practice page before making plans.

The organizer advertises a $3,000 pro purse, AMA Motocross Pro-Am points for A riders, and free InsiderMX coverage. Those are Motocross Pro-Am points, not a claim that this is a round of the professional AMA Supercross or SMX championship. The event homepage explains the program, and the organizer's viewing guide links the broadcasts.

The pro picture: follow-up, not another signing

Michael Mosiman’s Quad Lock Honda move remains October 22 news, covered in yesterday's Daily. MotoOnline’s later follow-up places him alongside Cameron McAdoo and Brodie Connolly in the team’s 250 program, with Christian Craig continuing in 450SMX. That is useful lineup context, not another batch of newly announced moves. MotoOnline's report

Our read: the next useful evidence is how these combinations prepare and race, not how many outlets repeat the same announcement.
