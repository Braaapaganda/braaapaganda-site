Braaapaganda Daily: Rick Ware Racing Joins ClubMX

A World Supercross partnership moves into AMA Supercross, the 2027 amateur Majors get dates, and Jaggar Townley adds a Mini O’s win.

November 26, 2026

Rick Ware Racing and ClubMX have worked together overseas. Now they’re taking that partnership into the U.S. paddock. The two announced a multiyear deal on November 25 to race as RWR ClubMX Yamaha in AMA Supercross beginning in 2027. The new name is official. The full lineup and how the two organizations will divide the work are not.

What changes for ClubMX

The team’s announcement, published by Racer X, explicitly names AMA Supercross and says the roster will come before the season. ClubMX owner Brandon Haas described the arrangement as more than a branding deal, and Rick Ware emphasized what the partnership can offer sponsors. Neither disclosed financial terms or who will make day-to-day sporting decisions. The release’s headline material also mentions Pro Motocross, but it does not spell out the outdoor program.

There is a racing result behind the partnership. In August, Max Anstie swept the three SX2 races at the World Supercross opener in Calgary on a Yamaha entered by Rick Ware Racing. In a September interview with World Supercross, Anstie credited the combination of the RWR crew and the bike and training facility at ClubMX. That was about the international program. The new agreement brings the relationship into AMA Supercross; it doesn’t tell us whether the U.S. operation will be run the same way.

In the media: MotoOnline’s Kane Taylor puts Anstie at the head of a group of riders already named by ClubMX. Anstie’s July signing announcement already committed him to the program through 2028. What to watch next is the team’s official 2027 lineup, including who lines up on each coast.

Six dates for the 2027 amateur Majors

MX Sports announced the amateur calendar on Wednesday, with FloRacing coverage planned for all six events. It starts at Daytona on March 7–9, then goes to Spring a Ding Ding on March 15–21, Mammoth on June 18–27, Loretta Lynn’s on August 2–7, Ponca City on October 6–10 and Mini O’s on November 20–27. Entry and race details will come from the individual events.

These are not pro national dates. They matter to the pro pipeline because the SMX League’s November 3 plan uses 250 A finishes at the Majors as a route into the following year’s regular-season SMX Next field. The 2026 races feed the 2027 field, not the events on this new calendar. The World All-Stars postseason is a separate step.

At Gatorback: Townley wins again

Vurbmoto’s Wednesday results and MX1Onboard list Jaggar Townley first in the Mini O’s 125 B/C Supercross main. It wasn’t his U.S. debut. He’d already won at Yamaha’s Alabama Invitational and at The Clash in Georgia earlier this month. In the separate 250 Pro Sport SX class, the later Vurbmoto table agrees with MX1Onboard’s published podium. We still couldn’t verify the organizer’s final SX sheet, so we’re leaving the discrepancy noted in Wednesday’s edition unresolved.

Direct Motocross’s Billy Rainford reported light rain and deepening ruts as outdoor motos got underway on Wednesday. The organizer’s format combines the SX main with the motocross overall for the Olympiad in classes that run both. Watch for the actual outdoor overalls as racing continues through Saturday. FloRacing’s event page has live and replay listings.
