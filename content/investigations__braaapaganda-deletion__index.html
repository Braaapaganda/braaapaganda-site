The accountability test

An appeal needs
an allegation.

The ethical case is strongest when it asks for concrete, testable safeguards.

  1. Identify the act. What video, link, product or conduct violated which rule?
  2. Explain the sanction. What made this severe or repeated, and why would a narrower remedy be insufficient?
  3. Make the review answerable. What evidence was considered, when, and how could the creator contest it?
  4. Preserve recovery. What happens to original recordings and the creator’s ability to retrieve them?

These are proposed standards of fairness, not findings of a legal violation. No right-of-reply request has been sent to either platform for this report.

Sources & method

Evidence first.
Uncertainty visible.

The case material

The investigation uses creator-supplied termination and appeal emails, a preservation request, screenshots and a full support conversation export, plus a note derived from original affiliate correspondence. These are correspondence records, not internal moderation logs.

Private originals, contact details, account identifiers and purchasing links are excluded from this public site. References E01–E08 identify retained evidence; they are not public download links.

The public-source checks

The report links official platform policies, transparency reporting, Rumble’s proposal and SEC disclosure, and voluntary civil-society principles. Current pages were checked October 28, 2026. The exact incident-date version of every policy was not authenticated.

TinyFish Deep Research supplied an initial research report. Direct source checks corrected material errors before this synthesis. ChatGPT’s separate Deep Research tool was not run.

What this investigation does not establish

It does not establish the precise violation, the original detection route, an automation-only appeal, a global channel false-positive rate, a motocross-wide purge, or that Rumble reliably offers better procedural protection. No independently verified set of major motocross termination cases was established.

How to read the private evidence references

E01: termination notice. E02: appeal decision. E03: clarification and preservation correspondence. E04–E06: overlapping screenshots of support and TeamYouTube exchanges. E07: full support transcript. E08: affiliate-correspondence research note. Overlapping screenshots are not independent corroboration.
