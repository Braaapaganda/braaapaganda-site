Braaapaganda Daily: Nationals Tickets and Newcastle’s Title Math

The full 2027 Pro Motocross ticket rollout starts today, and two races in Newcastle could produce two different SX2 champions.

November 27, 2026

Today is the first chance to shop the full 2027 Pro Motocross schedule in one day. Check the seller for your round before assuming every ticket is live. And if you’re watching the Australian Supercross finale from the U.S., it’s an overnight race with two different title tables to keep straight.

All 11 Nationals open their full ticket menus today

MX Sports Pro Racing announced November 23 that every 2027 outdoor round would open its full public ticket options on November 27. What’s different is the coordinated release: instead of waiting for individual tracks to open particular categories on different dates, fans can compare the whole season on the same day. That includes general admission and VIP, plus camping where the venue offers it. Feld had already announced regular-season ticket sales in October; what opens today is the full outdoor selection, not the first ticket for every race.

The Budds Creek ticket page, for example, specifies noon Eastern today for the public sale. That’s the August 21 outdoor race in Mechanicsville, Maryland. The season starts May 29 at Pala, runs at RedBud July 3 and wraps up August 28 at Ironman. Use the official schedule to find the seller and options for each track.

Newcastle: how one night could crown two SX2 champions

At Friday’s media day, MX Large’s posted account listed Christian Craig, Luke Clout, Cameron McAdoo and Max Anstie among the riders on hand. Craig brings a 44-point lead over Clout into the five-event AUSX standings. McAdoo is on 174 in SX2, ahead of Cole Davies (150), Brodie Connolly (145) and Anstie (144). Davies’ two-round cameo ended after Geelong, but his points still count. The November 15 post-Perth update is the context for those leads, not news that either title has been clinched. Honda’s November 8 report traces both riders’ title runs back through Geelong.

The wrinkle is that the four-round Australian championship leaves out Townsville. In that SX2 table Davies has 150, McAdoo 146 and Anstie 119. The published format calls for two points-paying finals apiece in SX1 and SX2, with 25 points for a win and 11 for tenth. Here’s the cleanest example: if Anstie wins twice and McAdoo finishes tenth twice, McAdoo still outscores Anstie 196–194 in the five-event series, but Anstie beats McAdoo 169–168 for the Australian title. Those are hypothetical totals, not predictions or race results.

In the media: MotoOnline’s Kane Taylor pointed out on November 26 that a single main wouldn’t have put enough points in play for the closest active challengers to catch either AUSX leader. The doubleheader changes that. MotoOnline also posted a Friday press-day podcast with Nic Still and Alex Gobert. The stadium program is listed for 2:15 a.m. Eastern Saturday, November 28. The promoter lists AUSX 360 for international live coverage and replay.

At Gatorback: wait for the outdoor overalls

Mini O’s motocross continues Friday and Saturday. The organizer’s scoring guide distinguishes a motocross overall from the Olympiad, which combines it with the Supercross result for eligible classes. Look for the final class sheets before reading a first-moto result as a title. FloRacing’s schedule lists a 7 a.m. Eastern broadcast on both days.
