Braaapaganda Daily: A Quiet Monday on the U.S. News Front

Monday’s source check finds no verified new U.S. race result or major team announcement, with the Geelong coverage already in the books.

November 9, 2026

Monday’s U.S. motocross and Supercross news check is a short one. No fresh national-series race result or confirmed major team announcement was verified in the sources reviewed by this edition’s cutoff.

The weekend coverage is already on the board

Our Saturday edition covered the Geelong race results, and Sunday’s Daily examined the championship standings. The November 8 race recap on Racer X concerns that same Saturday event. Its publication date does not make it another round of racing.

What would move the offseason picture?

The useful next developments will be confirmed rider assignments, published rule clarifications and new race information. The official SMX news page still led with its November 5 Insider episode when checked. Its recent SMX Next and WMX announcements remain relevant planning material, but were issued last week and have already appeared in the Daily.

For now, there is no verified new development here to justify another team-move headline or a third retelling of Geelong. This edition closes with that limited finding rather than treating offseason discussion as a completed deal.
