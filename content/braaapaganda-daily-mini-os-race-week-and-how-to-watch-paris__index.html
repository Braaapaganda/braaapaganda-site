Braaapaganda Daily: Mini O’s Race Week and How to Watch Paris

The Gatorback broadcast begins Sunday, Ian Harrison clarifies Sacha Coenen’s 250SX plans, and Paris has two daytime starts for U.S. viewers.

November 19, 2026

The next useful race weekend starts in two places: Gatorback for the U.S. amateur field and Paris for several riders preparing for American Supercross. The practical update this morning is how to follow both, and why Saturday’s Mini O’s practice matters even though the live video doesn’t start until Sunday.

Mini O’s: practice Saturday, live racing Sunday

The promoter’s schedule puts the 54th Mini O’s at Gatorback Cycle Park in Alachua, Florida, from November 21 to 28. Saturday opens with Supercross practice at 7 a.m. Eastern. FloRacing’s November 19 viewing guide lists its first live coverage on Sunday, November 22, at 7 a.m. Eastern, ahead of Supercross racing. Motocross practice follows Tuesday’s Supercross mains, with outdoor racing from Wednesday through Saturday. Vurbmoto’s viewing guide points to Prospect for live timing and results by class.

What to watch: In its November 16 report, Vurbmoto says timed laps set gate choice for the Supercross heats and first motocross motos. That is not the same as qualifying straight into a main. The promoter says heat and last-chance qualifier results set the Supercross main gate picks. Check practice for one-lap speed, then see who can turn a good gate into a result across both disciplines. The combined Olympiad awards reward riders who deliver on both tracks.

One team to follow: as we reported in October, Star Yamaha named Gatorback as the starting point for its new amateur group. Look at who actually lines up in each class rather than treating the team’s preseason class plans as final entries.

KTM follow-up: Sacha’s class, not his coast

After KTM’s roster launch, GateDrop’s published excerpts from Vital MX’s interview with team manager Ian Harrison add a distinction: Harrison says Sacha Coenen is slated for pro 250 Supercross, not an SMX Next warm-up program. That answers which level he’ll race, not which coast. His Paris SX2 entry is a chance to see him race indoors, not a preview of a confirmed matchup at Anaheim 1.

Paris: when to tune in

Plenitude Arena lists Saturday’s event at 1 p.m. Eastern, November 21, and Sunday’s at 8 a.m. Eastern, November 22. Those are venue times. Infront’s May 24 announcement confirms live MXGP-TV coverage for U.S. viewers; use the November 17 organizer entry list, rather than that spring release, for the current field. Watch how the riders handle starts and traffic over two days. Paris awards its own results, not AMA or SMX points.

In brief: Osborne joins FXR

In a November 18 release carried by Racer X, FXR announced a long-term apparel partnership with former 450 Pro Motocross champion Zach Osborne. The company pointed to his Vet Masters win the previous weekend in Georgia. This is an apparel announcement, not a return to the pro championship grid.

Research cutoff: November 19, 2026, 8:33 a.m. EST (13:33 UTC).
