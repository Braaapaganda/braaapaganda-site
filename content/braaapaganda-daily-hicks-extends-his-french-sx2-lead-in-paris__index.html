Braaapaganda Daily: Hicks Builds a 21-Point Lead as Roczen Sweeps Saturday in Paris

Hicks gained four points on his closest French SX2 rival, Roczen won all three Saturday races, and Mini O’s goes racing in Florida.

November 22, 2026

Michael Hicks has a bigger lead and one day of racing left. The Stark rider went into Paris 17 points up in the French SX2 championship and left Saturday 21 clear. On the same track, Jesson Turner won the open SX2 classification and Ken Roczen swept SX1. Those results tell three different stories.

Hicks is eighth in one table, second in another

Hicks finished 7–5–8 in the three races, good for eighth in the open Paris SX2 classification. Turner went 4–1–1 and won Superpole, finishing ahead of Cole Thompson and Izaih Clark in that Saturday table.

In the separate French SX Tour classification, Jules Piètre was first, Hicks second, Mickaël Lamarque third and Saad Soulimani fourth. The federation’s provisional season sheet puts Hicks at 102, Soulimani at 81, Lamarque at 74 and Piètre at 70. Hicks gained four points on Soulimani without winning a race or getting near the open podium. That’s why watching only the leaders on the broadcast can give you the wrong read on the title chase.

There’s a reason the margin matters to Hicks. The final 2025 French SX2 standings show Calvin Fonvieille beat him 143–137. This time he entered the last day with breathing room. In Sunday timed qualifying, Sacha Coenen was quickest, Piètre second, Hicks seventh and Soulimani ninth. That was pace, not another points result.

Roczen sweeps, and Triumph puts two bikes in the top five

The Saturday SX1 sheet gives Roczen all three wins and Superpole. Jason Anderson went 2–2–2, and Jordon Smith, Maxime Desprey and Aaron Plessinger rounded out the top five. Tom Vialle was sixth and Jo Shimoda seventh. This wasn't an AMA points race, but for riders switching bikes or classes, a full set of starts against this field tells us more than a preseason clip.

Media watch: Racer X carried Triumph’s Paris preview Friday, spotlighting Smith and Plessinger. Triumph had marked Paris as Plessinger’s competitive debut when it announced his signing September 30. Smith’s third and Plessinger’s fifth are a useful first benchmark for the two-bike effort, not a prediction for January. Sunday qualifying tightened the front: Anderson was 0.025 seconds behind Roczen, with Plessinger third and Smith fourth.

Sunday in progress: Coenen wins the first SX2 race

Sacha Coenen won the first open SX2 race ahead of Izaih Clark and Jesson Turner. Piètre was fourth and Hicks eighth. That open-race finish isn’t an updated French championship table. In SX1 Superpole, Roczen set the fastest lap again, with Smith second, Anderson third and Plessinger fourth.

What to watch next

Paris: Look for two separate sets of results: the weekend King and Prince classifications, and the French federation’s SX2 championship table. The provisional Sunday program puts the SX2 and SX1 finals at 10:54 and 11:11 a.m. Eastern. Saturday’s wins didn’t settle the weekend.

Florida: Saturday was practice at Gatorback. The Mini O’s organizer lists Sunday as the first Supercross race day, with motocross racing set to begin Wednesday. FloRacing’s timing guide points to Vurbmoto Prospect for class-by-class results. Early heat sheets are useful for matchups; save the championship claims for the mains and completed overalls.

Research cutoff: November 22, 2026, 8:55 a.m. EST (13:55 UTC). Sunday results were still in progress.
