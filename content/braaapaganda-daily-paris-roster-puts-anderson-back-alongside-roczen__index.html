Braaapaganda Daily: Paris Roster Puts Anderson Back Alongside Roczen

The November 11 provisional entry list adds detail to the Paris field, while an organizer statement resolves yesterday’s confirmation gap.

November 12, 2026

Thursday’s useful update is a clearer Paris Supercross field, not another new U.S. team deal. A provisional entry list dated November 11 puts Jason Anderson in SX1 alongside Ken Roczen, Jo Shimoda, Aaron Plessinger and Jordon Smith. For fans following the American championship’s offseason, it is a concrete look at who is expected to race together before the next AMA campaign.

From a reported replacement to a checked organizer statement

Yesterday’s Daily attributed Anderson’s replacement of Lucas Coenen to media reports because we could not retrieve a current organizer announcement. We have now checked the organizer’s November 10 statement. It says Anderson accepted the invitation following Coenen’s withdrawal and will race a HEP-prepared Suzuki RM-Z450, alongside Roczen.

That is a verification update, not a claim that the signing happened overnight. The statement confirms the substitution; it does not establish when Coenen will return to riding or how his recovery will affect his U.S. season.

What the November 11 list adds

The Paris press release and provisional roster published by Direct Motocross on November 11 list Shimoda in the 450cc SX1 class and Sacha Coenen in SX2. Anderson and Roczen are both listed on Suzuki, Plessinger and Smith on Triumph. The list is provisional, so treat it as the current plan rather than a guarantee that every rider will reach the gate.

The event remains November 21–22. The published program starts at 7 p.m. Saturday and 2 p.m. Sunday in Paris, equivalent to 1 p.m. and 8 a.m. Eastern Standard Time. Those are event-program times, not a verified U.S. broadcast schedule.

Why it matters

Our read: Paris offers a useful comparison across several riders and bikes familiar to U.S. fans, but an offseason result will still need context. Preparation, race format and the immediate target can differ from a full AMA campaign. The roster is a reason to watch, not a reason to declare a 2027 title favorite before anyone has raced.

The practical next steps are simple: look for final entries, rider preparation updates and an official viewing announcement. For Coenen, a direct riding-status update remains more informative than guessing from a missed appearance.

The media conversation

Coverage has moved from the initial replacement reports to a fuller event preview. The roster carried by Direct Motocross is press-release material, not an independent second confirmation of the organizer’s claims. We found no fresh domestic championship or rule announcement that justified padding this edition with repeated signings.
