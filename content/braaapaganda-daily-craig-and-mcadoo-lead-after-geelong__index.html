Braaapaganda Daily: Craig and McAdoo Lead the Chase After Geelong

The post-Geelong AUSX standings put Christian Craig 32 points clear in SX1 and Cameron McAdoo just two ahead in SX2. Plus, the latest post-race listening.

November 8, 2026

Sunday’s follow-up to Geelong is the championship picture. Yesterday’s Daily covered the Roczen and Davies sweeps; today, the series standings show what those results mean for Christian Craig, Cameron McAdoo and the riders chasing them. No new U.S. national-series race result or confirmed major team announcement was verified in the sources reviewed by this edition’s cutoff.

Craig carries a 32-point cushion

Computime’s provisional Boost Mobile AUSX SX1 series standings put Craig on 182 points, followed by Ken Roczen on 150 and Luke Clout on 147. These are cumulative championship totals, separate from the Geelong round scores published yesterday.

Craig’s three runner-up finishes added 66 points. Roczen collected 75, so Craig surrendered nine points to him across the event while retaining the series lead. The arithmetic gives the American Honda rider something useful to protect heading into the final two rounds, even after another dominant night from Roczen.

McAdoo and Davies separated by two

The tighter contest is SX2. The official timing provider’s provisional series sheet lists McAdoo first on 152, Davies second on 150 and Lux Turner third on 138. Max Anstie is fifth on 119, behind Brodie Connolly’s 129.

That leaves McAdoo with almost no breathing room. Davies earned the full 75 at Geelong while McAdoo added 58. Both championship sheets remain labeled provisional, so these figures should be read with that qualification.

Perth next; post-race voices available

The AUSX organizer’s release published by Racer X on November 8 confirms the series heads to Perth’s HBF Park on November 14, before the Newcastle finale. That release concerns Saturday’s racing, not a new Sunday event.

For readers following the U.S.-series regulars overseas, MotoOnline’s November 7 post-race interview package lists Jo Shimoda, Craig, Roczen, McAdoo, Turner and Davies. Its November 8 recap podcast adds a review from Nic Still and Alex Gobert. Those are listening and viewing links; this edition does not attribute statements to unreviewed recordings.

Coverage note: Written sources and official timing sheets establish the facts above. Racer X, Vital MX, MotoOnline US and PulpMX were checked. Swapmoto Live returned an access error; Cooksey and Hopper video content could not be reviewed reliably, so no claims from those recordings are included. Previously covered rule changes, team negotiations and product announcements have not been repackaged as new news.
