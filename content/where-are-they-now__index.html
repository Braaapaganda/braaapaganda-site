How it works

  1. Send a short introduction. Use the email subject Where Are They Now? interview request in your email. Tell us briefly what you would like to discuss and provide a reply email.

  2. Decide whether to take part. A request does not commit you to appearing or sharing medical details. It does not guarantee an episode.

  3. Agree the details separately. If an interview goes ahead, recording and publication permission will be agreed with you before participating.

Please keep sensitive documents out of your email. Do not send medical records, identity documents or insurance account details. This is an interview request, not an application for rider benefits or a medical or emergency service.
