Chase Sexton and Jorge Prado have taken plenty of blame. But Kawasaki’s results, rider feedback and public disputes point to a bigger problem inside the team.

We look at Jason Anderson’s breakout 2022 season, the setup mistakes in 2023, the current KX450 platform and how riders performed before, during and after Kawasaki. Then we compare what the riders said with how team personnel responded.

My take: Kawasaki has serious changes to make. Sexton and Prado brought their own problems, but putting them into this team, on this bike, created a perfect storm. The evidence points to a team-performance problem, not proof that one part caused everything.

Would another rider fix Kawasaki, or does Kawasaki need to fix what surrounds the rider?
